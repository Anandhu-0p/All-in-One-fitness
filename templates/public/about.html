About us

One platform for your whole fitness journey

All-in-One Fitness connects members with certified trainers and health experts, and puts workouts, nutrition, attendance, payments, events and progress tracking in a single, simple place.

Our mission

Make consistent, guided fitness accessible — so every member has a coach, a plan and a community, not just an app.

Privacy first

Health data is sensitive. Only you, your assigned trainer and administrators can see it — never the public.

Human coaches

Every member is allocated to a real batch with a real trainer, and can chat with nutrition and wellness experts.
